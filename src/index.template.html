How to play

Guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries. Guesses must be real words.
2

3
Tile colors show how close each guess is:

4
A new word drops every 12 hours — one AM round, one PM round, same for everyone.
5

6
Add your name to join the group board, then hit Share to post your result.
Ranking rules
🥇
Whoever solves in the fewest guesses wins the round. If two players tie on guesses, whoever took less time to solve ranks higher — timed from your first guess to your winning guess, not from when the round started, so it doesn't matter when you open the app.
🥈
The top 3 by that rule get a gold/silver/bronze medal — always based on this rule, even while you're viewing the board sorted by Time.
🥉
The Time / Turns toggle only changes what order the board is displayed in — Time shows who finished first chronologically, Turns shows fewest guesses (then fastest solve time) first. Medals don't move.
📱
A small device icon with a number means that name has been played from more than one device — anyone can play under any name, so this is just an FYI, not a lock.
